# Selah

A daily devotional journal that runs in the browser. Plan Bible passages on a calendar, then reflect on each day with the S.E.L.A.H. flow: Scripture, Examine, Lesson, Acknowledge, Heart. Pages can be highlighted and decorated with writing tools, drawing, stickers and photos.

**Open it:** https://aplovec23.github.io/selah/

- Planning a day downloads its passage (World English Bible and King James Version from bible-api.com, and the Tagalog Ang Dating Biblia 1905 from getBible.net, all public domain) and saves it in your browser. After that, the day opens with no internet.
- The Cebuano Ang Biblia, Bugna version (1917, public domain, from the Christian Classics Ethereal Library) is built in and works offline.
- You can also paste a passage yourself from your own Bible app or a website.
- ESV and NLT can be added in Settings with your own free keys from the publishers (api.esv.org, api.nlt.to).
- Everything you write stays in your browser on that device. Use Settings → Export backup to move it.
- Pages can have moving backgrounds (drawn live, no video files) or your own looping video/GIF.
- A music player (♪) plays your own songs or a Spotify playlist while you journal.
- Add it to your home screen ("Add to Home Screen" / "Install app") to use it like an app.

The only Bible text in this repository is the public-domain Cebuano 1917 Bible (`data/ceb1917.json`). No copyrighted Bible text is included.
